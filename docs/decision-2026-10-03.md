## Decision

**Option D, with conditions.**

1. **Fix the two open demand defects first** (dsh audit, `results/bounded/audit_demand_dsh.md`):
   - B1: corridor gates carry demand without justification.
   - B2: the congested gate `1189103201` is fitted at its 3,600 veh/h cap.

   Every gate is recorded as *measured*, *inferred* (flow balance) or *scenario-assumed* with a declared range. Unknown arrivals are neither measured demand nor automatically zero.
2. **Choose the baseline demand scale by rule, not by taste.** Use the largest scale at which a 7,200-s Proxy run on `net_bounded_v4` meets the gates below. Evidence that this is attainable: the isolated v4 test at 50% gate demand had 0 pending (`results/bounded/netv4/netv4_report.md` §3).
3. **Baseline acceptance gates** (from Astra; "reduced demand alone is insufficient"):
   - zero teleports;
   - departures, arrivals, running and pending vehicles reconcile;
   - no unexplained growing insertion queue;
   - the Proxy incident is observable in the Twin's sparse detectors;
   - at least one intervention has an effect larger than paired seed variability, and the VMS reaches a meaningful number of vehicles (D1–D4 reached only 4–5).
4. **Fallback trigger.** If the baseline cannot meet the gates by the end of 2026-10-04, build the small curated corridor (option B-lite): the two routes, the incident segment, detector ordering, the diversion and rejoin, and the relevant signals, labelled as a simplified research model. No further junction-repair campaign (option A beyond B1/B2).
5. **Mesoscopic mode (C): not adopted now.** dsh proposed a one-run probe; Astra and I are against migrating. The core method depends on lane-level incidents (stopped vehicles, lane closures), E1 detectors, and snapshot/branching semantics that have only been characterised in microscopic mode. Reopen C only if B-lite also fails, or for the December final.
6. **Claims discipline** (the judge attack all four raised: "a synthetic world tuned to make the method work"):
   - Separate geographic/data grounding from calibrated realism.
   - Keep the Proxy/Twin mechanisms independently varied.
   - Include congestion and demand-shock controls and untouched test seeds.
   - Report negative results.
   - Set an explicit calibration milestone before the 12-26 final.
   - No real-world benefit claims.
